# Sources and Verification Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24.

This playbook is practical professional guidance, not legal, HR, financial, regulatory, data-protection, or professional-body advice. Professional duties vary by sector, jurisdiction, employer, client contract, tool, account type, and data class.

## AI Risk and Governance

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - voluntary framework for managing AI risks to individuals, organisations, and society.
- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) - describes Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage as continuous AI risk-management functions.
- [ICO: AI and data protection guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/about-this-guidance/) - UK guidance on AI systems that process personal data, including fairness, lawfulness, transparency, rights, security, and risk management.

## Data Handling and Tool Tiers

- [OpenAI: Business data privacy, security, and compliance](https://openai.com/business-data/) - describes OpenAI's business-data commitments, including business-product training defaults, encryption, retention controls, security, DPAs, and data residency.
- [OpenAI: How your data is used to improve model performance](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) - explains differences between individual services and business products for model-improvement data use.
- [OpenAI: Security and privacy](https://openai.com/security-and-privacy/) - overview of individual and business security and privacy controls.

## Claims to Re-check Often

- Business vs individual AI-product data-use defaults.
- Retention, deletion, human-review, memory, connected-app, and feedback settings.
- Employer policies, client contracts, professional-body rules, and sector-specific duties.
- Whether an AI tool is approved for client, employee, personal, financial, legal, health, confidential, or regulated data.
- Whether an output is client-facing, decision-shaping, or relied on for legal, financial, HR, safety, or compliance purposes.
