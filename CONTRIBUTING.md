# Contributing

This playbook gets better when professionals who've crossed the Integrator–Architect gap share what actually moved them. Welcome contributions:

## What's welcome

- **Corrections** — typos, broken links, unclear steps.
- **Source updates** — changed vendor policies, data-handling terms, regulator guidance, professional-body rules, or internal-reference links.
- **Role paths** — a role not yet covered, or a sharper Improve/Create cut for one that is. Open an issue first.
- **"What moved me" stories** — the peer, the question, or the permission that got you unstuck. These are the point of the model.
- **Translations** — faithful, credited.

## What isn't

- Anything that reframes the barrier as purely a skill gap — the model's whole claim is that it usually isn't.
- Vendor or affiliate placements.
- Client, employer, employee, HR, legal, financial, health, confidential, regulated, proprietary, credential, or personal information in public issues or PRs.

## Standards

Plain, specific, UK English. No hype. Use public or synthetic examples unless the case is already public. Claims about tool behaviour, data handling, professional duty, or safety should use primary or high-authority sources where possible. Open an issue before large changes; PRs reviewed as promptly as a one-person team reasonably can.
