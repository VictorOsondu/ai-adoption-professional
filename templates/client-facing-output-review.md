## Review Checklist

- [ ] The intended human owner has read the full output.
- [ ] All facts, numbers, dates, names, citations, sources, and quotes are verified.
- [ ] Legal, financial, tax, HR, medical, safety, or compliance statements have the right specialist review.
- [ ] The output does not make unauthorised commitments, promises, prices, discounts, refunds, eligibility decisions, or policy exceptions.
- [ ] The tone fits the audience and brand.
- [ ] Confidential, personal, client, employee, or regulated information is handled appropriately.
- [ ] The output does not reveal internal strategy, negotiation position, private methodology, or sensitive assumptions.
- [ ] Any AI-use disclosure required by policy, client terms, publisher rules, or professional duty is included.
- [ ] The output is accessible and clear.
- [ ] A record is kept if the output is material.

## Common Failure Patterns

- Confident but unsupported claim.
- Fabricated citation or source.
- Generic advice presented as tailored professional advice.
- Commitment beyond authority.
- Private information included by accident.
- Tone that sounds plausible but not like the organisation.
- Missing caveat, limitation, or escalation route.

## Decision

- [ ] Send.
- [ ] Revise.
- [ ] Specialist review needed.
