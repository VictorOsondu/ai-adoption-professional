# Role Paths — where to start in your job

Quick-start cuts of the [ICE Method](six-stages-for-professionals.md#the-ice-method-for-your-role) by role. Each gives you a safe first **Improve** win (do this week) and a **Create** idea (when you're ready to build). Every path shares the same guardrail at the bottom.

## Marketer / comms

- **Improve:** first drafts of posts, briefs, and campaign variants; repurposing one asset into five; summarising research.
- **Create:** a reusable brief template, a bank of on-brand prompt patterns, an A/B variant generator for your channel.
- **Watch:** brand voice drifts to generic. Read it aloud — does it sound like you, or like any brand?

## Manager / team lead

- **Improve:** meeting notes into actions, first-draft updates, structuring a plan, prepping one-to-ones.
- **Create:** an onboarding guide for your team, a decision-log template, a shared prompt library for the team.
- **Watch:** never let AI make or shape decisions about people (hiring, performance). Draft the job ad; own the decision.

## Operations / admin

- **Improve:** SOP drafts, process documentation, spreadsheet formula help, turning messy notes into structure.
- **Create:** a self-serve FAQ, a checklist library, a lightweight internal knowledge base.
- **Watch:** verify every process step and number — confident and wrong is the default failure.

## Customer-facing / support

- **Improve:** draft replies (then personalise), summarising a case history, tone adjustments for tricky messages.
- **Create:** a response-pattern library for common issues, a macro set, a "how we sound" voice guide.
- **Watch:** no customer personal data in public tools; a human reads every reply before it sends.

## HR / people

- **Improve:** first-draft policies, job descriptions, interview question sets, summarising engagement feedback themes.
- **Create:** a manager toolkit, an onboarding checklist, a plain-English policy library.
- **Watch:** no personal, disciplinary, or health data in any AI tool; no AI-driven decisions about individuals.

## Analyst / knowledge worker

- **Improve:** explaining a dataset, drafting the narrative around findings, literature and background scans, code help.
- **Create:** a repeatable reporting template, a methods explainer, a small analysis tool.
- **Watch:** AI invents plausible statistics and citations. Every number and source gets traced before it ships.

## Educator / trainer

- **Improve:** lesson and session plans, differentiated materials, practice questions, feedback drafts.
- **Create:** a course outline, a resource bank, an assessment redesign that AI can't shortcut.
- **Watch:** keep AI on the *support* side of learning, never the side that does the thinking for the learner.

---

## The shared guardrail (every role)

Run the **ABC Method** on any AI task: **Aim** (finish "I'm using AI because I want to ___"), **Brief** (give it the context it needs — and only what you're allowed to share), **Check** (is it accurate, appropriate, safe?).

**Never** put into a public AI tool: confidential information, personal data, contracts, financial records, or anything you wouldn't be comfortable seeing on a public website.
