# Role Paths — Where to Start in Your Job

Quick-start cuts of the [ICE Method](../framework/six-stages-for-professionals.md#the-ice-method-for-your-role) by role. Each gives you a safe first **Improve** win, a **Create** idea, and a **Protect** watchpoint.

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

## Marketer / Comms

- **Improve:** first drafts of posts, briefs, and campaign variants; repurposing one asset into five; summarising public research.
- **Create:** a reusable brief template, a bank of on-brand prompt patterns, an A/B variant generator for your channel.
- **Protect:** brand voice drifts to generic. Check claims, permissions, copyright, sensitive launches, and client approvals before publishing.

## Manager / Team Lead

- **Improve:** meeting notes into actions, first-draft updates, structuring a plan, prepping one-to-ones.
- **Create:** an onboarding guide for your team, a decision-log template, a shared prompt library for the team.
- **Protect:** do not let AI make or materially shape hiring, performance, disciplinary, redundancy, or promotion decisions. Drafting support is not decision authority.

## Operations / Admin

- **Improve:** SOP drafts, process documentation, spreadsheet formula help, turning messy notes into structure.
- **Create:** a self-serve FAQ, a checklist library, a lightweight internal knowledge base.
- **Protect:** verify every process step, number, owner, deadline, and dependency. Do not paste credentials, private links, or internal access details into public tools.

## Customer-Facing / Support

- **Improve:** draft replies, summarise a case history, adjust tone for tricky messages.
- **Create:** a response-pattern library for common issues, a macro set, a "how we sound" voice guide.
- **Protect:** no customer personal data in public tools. A human reviews every reply. AI must not make prices, refunds, eligibility decisions, policy exceptions, promises, legal statements, or commitments without authority.

## HR / People

- **Improve:** first-draft policies, job descriptions, interview question sets, summarising anonymised engagement themes.
- **Create:** a manager toolkit, an onboarding checklist, a plain-English policy library.
- **Protect:** no employee personal, disciplinary, health, grievance, performance, or protected-characteristic data in public tools. Approved HR systems only, with policy, legal, DPO, and human-review controls.

## Analyst / Knowledge Worker

- **Improve:** explaining a dataset, drafting the narrative around findings, public literature and background scans, code help.
- **Create:** a repeatable reporting template, a methods explainer, a small analysis tool.
- **Protect:** trace every number, source, quote, method, formula, and assumption before it ships. Test code and calculations independently.

## Legal / Compliance

- **Improve:** first drafts of non-client-specific checklists, issue lists, policy summaries, and research plans.
- **Create:** a matter-opening checklist, compliance obligations tracker, source-verification workflow, or client-intake triage template.
- **Protect:** no privileged, client, confidential, litigation, investigation, or regulated material in public tools. Verify every authority against primary sources. AI output is not legal advice.

## Finance / Accounting

- **Improve:** spreadsheet formula help, variance narrative drafts, public benchmark summaries, checklist drafts.
- **Create:** a month-end close checklist, budget narrative template, assumptions register, or finance FAQ.
- **Protect:** no client, payroll, tax, bank, forecast, investor, or material non-public information in public tools. Recalculate figures independently and document assumptions.

## Consultant / Adviser

- **Improve:** workshop agendas, discovery questions, synthesis of non-confidential notes, proposal structure.
- **Create:** a diagnostic checklist, client-readiness scorecard, reusable workshop canvas, or implementation tracker.
- **Protect:** do not paste client confidential data into public tools. Check contract terms, attribution, methodology, and whether AI-generated recommendations are being over-sold.

## Sales / Business Development

- **Improve:** account research from public sources, call prep, first-draft follow-ups, objection handling.
- **Create:** qualification checklist, discovery-question bank, proposal outline, account-plan template.
- **Protect:** no confidential pricing, negotiation strategy, customer personal data, or non-public pipeline data in public tools. AI must not offer terms, discounts, or commitments without approval.

## Product / Engineering

- **Improve:** code explanation, test ideas, documentation drafts, bug triage summaries, design-option comparison.
- **Create:** internal dev helper, test-case generator, release-note workflow, lightweight support tool.
- **Protect:** no secrets, keys, credentials, proprietary code, customer data, or vulnerability details in public tools. Review generated code for security, licensing, and correctness.

## Researcher / Writer

- **Improve:** outline options, language editing, public literature scanning, argument stress-testing.
- **Create:** source matrix, citation-check workflow, research-notes template, publication checklist.
- **Protect:** AI is not an author. Disclose use where required. Do not upload unpublished, peer-review, participant, funder, or confidential material where confidentiality cannot be assured.

## Educator / Trainer

- **Improve:** lesson and session plans, differentiated materials, practice questions, feedback drafts.
- **Create:** a course outline, a resource bank, an assessment redesign that AI cannot shortcut.
- **Protect:** keep AI on the support side of learning. Do not use public tools for learner personal data, safeguarding, welfare, or assessed work with identifying detail.

## Executive Assistant / Chief of Staff

- **Improve:** agenda drafts, briefing packs from approved material, action summaries, travel or meeting logistics.
- **Create:** decision-log system, board-pack checklist, stakeholder brief template, recurring meeting workflow.
- **Protect:** no board-confidential, HR, legal, M&A, financial, strategy, investor, or sensitive personal data in public tools. Check distribution lists and authority before sending.

## The Shared Guardrail

Run the **ABC + Protect Method** on any AI task:

- **Aim:** finish "I'm using AI because I want to ___."
- **Brief:** give it the context it needs and only what you are allowed to share.
- **Check:** is it accurate, appropriate, safe, and fit for the audience?
- **Protect:** what data, duty, decision, authority, or disclosure issue is involved?

**Public tools:** never enter confidential information, personal data, client data, contracts, financial records, HR material, credentials, legal material, health information, unpublished strategy, proprietary code, or anything you would not be comfortable seeing on a public website.

**Approved work tools:** still check policy, settings, data class, contract terms, retention, sharing, connected apps, and human-review requirements.
